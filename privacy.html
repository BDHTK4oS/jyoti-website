Privacy policy

How JyotiCab.com (Jyoti Travels) collects, uses and protects the details you share with us. Last updated 6 November 2026 (visitor statistics added).

Who we are

This website, jyoticab.com, is run by Jyoti Travels (trading as JyotiCab.com), 2076, Rajdanga Main Road, Kasba, Kolkata 700107, West Bengal, India. We are responsible for the personal data you give us through this website, and we follow the Information Technology Act, 2000, its Reasonable Security Practices and Sensitive Personal Data or Information Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as it comes into force.

What we collect

  • Enquiry and corporate forms: your name, phone number, email address (if given), company name (corporate form), travel date and time, pickup and drop addresses, the car you want and any notes you write.
  • Fare estimator: the pickup, drop and stop points you choose on the map. If you tap "Use my location", your browser asks your permission and then shares your current position with the page; it is used only to place the pickup pin.
  • Phone and WhatsApp: if you call or message us, we see your number and what you tell us, as with any phone call or chat.
  • Technical data: like every website, our hosting provider and the services listed below receive your IP address and browser details when pages load.
  • Visitor statistics (only if you accept cookies): if you click "Accept" on the cookie message, Zoho SalesIQ records your visit: the pages you view, how you reached our site (for example Google or a WhatsApp link), your approximate location from your IP address, and your device and browser. If you then send an enquiry, it is linked to that visit. If you click "Decline", none of this is recorded. We do not use advertising trackers.

We do not ask for passwords, bank or card details, or government ID numbers on this website.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, quote a fare, and confirm and run your booking.
  • To raise invoices and keep the business and tax records the law requires.
  • To keep the website secure and stop spam.
  • If you accepted cookies, to understand which pages and channels bring us enquiries, so we can improve the website.

We use your details only for these purposes. We do not sell them or share them for marketing.

Who helps us run the website

These service providers handle data for us, only to provide their service. Some of them store or process data outside India.

  • Netlify hosts the website and stores the form entries you send.
  • Web3Forms forwards a copy of each form entry to our email inbox.
  • Google provides the maps, the place suggestions while you type an address, road distances for the fare estimate, and the Google reviews shown on the site. What you type in an address box and the points you place on the map are sent to Google for this. Google's use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Zoho (Zoho SalesIQ, Zoho CRM and Zoho Analytics) stores visitor statistics if you accept cookies, and our enquiries and customer records. See the Zoho Privacy Policy.
  • Yahoo Mail hosts our business email inbox.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) and Google Fonts deliver the animation library and fonts the pages use.

We may also share details where the law requires it, for example with tax authorities or in answer to a lawful request.

Cookies

The website only sets cookies for visitor statistics, and only after you click "Accept" on the cookie message. Your choice is remembered on your device. To change it, clear this website's data in your browser and choose again when the message reappears.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and booking, and booking and invoice records for as long as tax and accounting laws require. After that we delete them.

How we protect it

The website is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Form entries are kept in accounts that only we can sign in to, and only the people who handle bookings can see them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, or delete them (unless the law requires us to keep them), and you can withdraw your consent at any time. You can also choose not to give us information, though we may then be unable to take your booking. To do any of this, contact our grievance officer below.
