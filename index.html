How a duty is billed

No surprises on the invoice. Every duty follows the same five steps, and your traveller signs the readings.

  2. 1

    Garage out

    The car leaves our garage in Kasba. Time and kilometres for the duty start here.

  3. 2

    Reporting

    Your traveller writes the reporting time and kilometres on the duty slip.

  4. 3

    On duty

    The first 10 hours and 100 km are covered by the package price.

  5. 4

    Release

    Your traveller writes the release time and kilometres in their own hand.

  6. 5

    Garage in

    Extra hours or extra km are charged, whichever comes to more. Never both.
